Two Sources — One Answer

Your .ipynb built argument trees from literature. data.xlsx records clinical validation of biomarkers. Neither is complete alone — this platform links them.

🌳
Argument Tree (.ipynb)
NLP extraction from PubMed sepsis papers using rule-based (v3) and LLM (v4) methods. Captures claims, evidence polarity, and scientific conflicts.
⚠ Lacks quantification: lactate predicts shock — but AUC? Cutoff? Which population?
📊
Clinical Database (data.xlsx)
875 biomarkers across neonatal, adult and pediatric populations. Each entry includes AUC, sensitivity, specificity, cutoff values, and drug targets.
⚠ Lacks clinical logic: numbers exist in isolation — no guidance on which clinical decision they support.
The Bridge: Claim → Biomarker → Quantification
Every claim mentions an intervention or mechanism. The database has matching biomarkers. Example: the RCT conflict on steroids (ADRENAL vs APROCCHSS) is resolved by IL-6 (AUC 1.00) + HLA-DR (AUC 0.97) identifying the steroid-sensitive subgroup.
